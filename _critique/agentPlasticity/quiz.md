# Comprehension quiz (questions only)

A short video claims to explain one research paper. This quiz checks what a viewer actually learns from the video alone.
Answer from the video kit only. If the video did not tell you, write `not conveyed`. Do not guess from outside knowledge, and say when you did guess.

1. In two sentences, what is this video about?
2. According to the video, what is missing in the usual way of testing AI agents, and what does this paper add?
3. What does the AI agent change between one game and the next, and what does it NOT change?
4. The video uses the word "artifacts". In your own words, what are they? Give an example the video showed.
5. What is "plasticity" in plain words? What is counted, and what is it compared with or divided by?
6. Why does the test score the agent on games it did not practise on (the video calls them "held-out")?
7. What was the paper's main finding about the different AI models?
8. The video compares two kinds of "best". What are they, and does the video say the same model wins both? Use the video's own example.
9. According to the video, why did some agents improve less than others?
10. What should a viewer NOT conclude from the numbers in the video?
11. Which sentence or picture in the video confused you most, and why?
12. Explain the paper to a friend in three sentences, using only what this video told you.

Also give, at the end:

- A rating from 1 to 5 for each: (a) the opening told me what the video was about, (b) the story had a clear beginning, middle and end, (c) every new word was explained before it was used, (d) after one viewing I could explain the paper to someone else.
- A line-by-line log: for each line id, mark `clear`, `partly` or `lost`, and give one short reason.
