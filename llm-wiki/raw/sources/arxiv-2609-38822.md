# What the abstract says

- Open-source aggregations hold more than 230,000 agent skills, so selection is the bottleneck.[^abs]
- The standing approach lets the LLM select skills in a retrieval loop that rewrites queries and refines candidates, paying LLM tokens on every task.[^abs]
- SkillSeek is an open-source two-stage retriever: a BGE-base bi-encoder feeding a small cross-encoder, exposed over MCP.[^abs]
- Evaluated on the 89-task SkillsBench benchmark over a 4 x 11 grid of pool, backbone and method. It reaches observed parity with the LLM-mediated loop of Liu et al.[^abs]
- Plain BM25 alone matches or beats the refined loop's pass rate on three of four settings. A small cross-encoder covers the remaining difference on the fourth.[^abs]
- Per-trial spend drops from USD 51.30 to USD 27.54, within fifty cents of the no-skill baseline.[^abs]
- Scope stated by the authors: SkillsBench tasks and the OpenHands harness.[^abs]
