# What the paper says

- Problem (Abstract, sec 1): models ship with thinking modes, but "no-thinking" is usually inferred from proxies (a disabled thinking mode, no `<think>` block, a short output). "These proxies are unreliable: disabled thinking modes still emit extended reasoning, or long traces often contain uninformative filler rather than genuine inference."[^p1][^p2]
- Framework (sec 2.2, 2.3): each response is split into visible pre-answer text T and the final answer A. Three levels: Empty-Thinking Rate (ETR, strict answer-only compliance, T empty and A parseable); Question-Pre-answer Relevance (QRel., cosine similarity from the instruction-aware Qwen3-Embedding-4B, empty T scores 0); Explicit Inference Rate (EIR, a blinded LLM judge labels non-empty T with one of five labels; only "Explicit reasoning" counts: "deduction, computation, comparison, elimination, rule application, evidence-to-conclusion, or causality addressing Q"). The judge sees only (Q, T). All rates use all outputs as the denominator.[^p4][^p5][^p6]
- Scope statement (sec 2.1, 2.2, footnote 1): "no-thinking is used as a response-level concept, distinct from latent reasoning processes within the LLM"; the decomposition is made "without making claims about task-relevant computation in unobserved model states".[^p2][^p4]
- Modes (Table 2, sec 2.4): M1 THINK-ON; M2 THINK-OFF (thinking disabled, no prompt change, "the natural baseline"); M3 COT ("Think step by step."); M4 SOFT ("A very short lead-in is allowed, but do not explain."); M5 STRICT ("Give the final answer directly without any reasoning."); M6 PREFIX (prefills "The answer is \boxed{"). All modes share the same task stem and final-answer request.[^p7]
- Models (Table 3, Table B.2): Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-32B (local, full splits), Qwen3-235B-A22B, DeepSeek-V4-Flash (284B), GPT-5.4, Gemini-3-Flash(-Preview). For Modes 2 to 6 thinking is off or none for five models; Gemini-3-Flash runs at "minimal", which "is treated as near-off rather than strict off". Temperature 0, 8,192 max tokens for all modes.[^p8][^p21]
- Benchmarks (sec 3.1, Table B.1): Boolean (BoolQ, StrategyQA), multiple choice (MMLU, MMLU-Pro), open-ended (GSM8K, MATH). API models use 1,000 sampled items per benchmark (all 687 for StrategyQA); the local Qwen3-4B and 32B runs use full splits. The audits cover 572,688 outputs. The judge is GPT-5.5 at temperature 0; Claude Opus 4.8 replicates Level 3 on the full corpus.[^p8][^p21][^p23][^p26]
- Native think-off (sec 3.2, Figure 4): under M2, most models reach roughly 80 to 100% ETR on Bool; MCQ is "intermediate but heterogeneous" (ETR near zero to 95%); on open-ended tasks "ETR collapses to nearly zero everywhere" and EIR "remains high". Italic summary: "We use 'thinking inertia' to describe how visible explicit inference persists under no-thinking controls and becomes more common as the answer space opens." M3 re-elicits visible reasoning; GPT-5.4 is the main exception, keeping high answer-only compliance on Bool and MCQ.[^p8][^p9]
- Pooled EIR (Table D.3, GPT-5.5 judge, all outputs): M2 Bool 5.15%, MCQ 45.57%, Open 99.69%; M5 Bool 0.12%, MCQ 25.39%, Open 62.00%. Claude Opus 4.8: M2 4.24%, 40.73%, 98.55%; M5 0.10%, 24.21%, 61.02%.[^p23]
- Per model M2 EIR (Figure H.1, Table H.2): Bool / MCQ / Open = Qwen3-235B 12.2 / 68.7 / 99.7; DeepSeek 5.5 / 81.0 / 99.9; GPT-5.4 0.0 / 5.3 / 99.5; Gemini 24.2 / 88.9 / 99.7; Qwen3-4B 1.26 / 49.30 / 99.86; Qwen3-32B 0.00 / 37.11 / 99.56. M5 Open EIR for Qwen3-4B 97.78%, Qwen3-32B 56.73%, Qwen3-235B 98.90% (H.2). No per-model M5 EIR is given for the four API models; G.2 gives their M5 ETR (GPT-5.4: 100 and 99.8 on GSM8K and MATH).[^p31][^p32][^p33]
- Trade-off (sec 1, sec 3.3, Figure 5): "across the five LLMs with fully disabled thinking, strict answer-only prompting raises mean ETR from near zero to roughly 40% while reducing mean accuracy by about 15 percentage points" on open-ended tasks. "Bool and MCQ show little corresponding accuracy trade-off." Bold run-in: "On open-ended tasks, no-thinking comes at a cost." Per-model accuracy change M2 to M5 (G.2), GSM8K: Qwen3-4B +0.1, Qwen3-32B -49.0, Qwen3-235B +0.9, DeepSeek -25.1, GPT -22.7; MATH: +0.5, -6.2, -1.8, -16.9, -28.5 (Gemini +11.0 and +12.6, but its M5 ETR is 0.0 everywhere).[^p3][^p10][^p11][^p31]
- Prefix forcing (sec 3.3): M6 is not a stronger STRICT; mean ETR drops from M5 to M6 on MCQ and open-ended, and QRel. and EIR rebound.[^p11]
- Domains (sec 3.4, Table 4): under fixed four-choice MMLU, STEM combines the highest accuracy with the lowest ETR (0.3 to 36.4%) and the highest EIR (59.7 to 85.5%) for each of four models.[^p11][^p12]
- Answer-space rewrite (sec 3.5, Figure 6, Table I.5): matched numeric-answer MMLU/MMLU-Pro items for Qwen3-32B under M2. Boolean rewrite: accuracy 77.8 to 78.9%, ETR 10.5 to 62.5%, EIR 86.95 to 36.90%. Open-ended rewrite: accuracy 52.6%, ETR 0%, EIR 90.14%. Italic summary: "Even for the same question, the answer space shapes whether an LLM answers directly."[^p11][^p12][^p37]
- Full-page example (p. 13): one MMLU geometry item (rectangle, length twice the width, diagonal 5√5, area 50) rewritten as Boolean, MCQ and open-ended, Qwen3-32B in all six modes. Under M5 the Boolean and MCQ forms return only the boxed answer (green check); the open-ended form writes the full Pythagorean solution.[^p13]
- Human study (sec 3.6, D.3): 600 outputs, five annotators. Human explicit-inference labels reach 98.8% mean binary agreement with GPT-5.5 and 96.7% with Opus 4.8. Human mean rates: M2 Open 100.0%, M5 Open 57.1%.[^p14][^p25]
- Limitations (sec 5.1): "Our claims therefore concern visible output, not the absence of task-relevant computation from hidden states." Multilingual, code, tool-use, multi-turn and stochastic settings, and drift in proprietary endpoints, remain open. EIR depends on the rubric and judge calibration.[^p15]
- Implications (sec 5.2): suggests "task-aware routing: answer directly when the interface provides sufficient support, and preserve explicit computation when suppression would sacrifice accuracy." "Visible compliance should not, however, be equated with lower inference cost when hidden reasoning may remain active."[^p16]
